GiggleMe and the Guild Members Intent

GiggleMe requests Discord's Guild Members Privileged Gateway Intent for member and role placeholder resolution in scheduled messages.

Why GiggleMe uses placeholders

GiggleMe allows a scheduled message to contain placeholders that are resolved later, when the message is actually delivered.

{Testers}
{Testers:expand}

1. Preventing premature mentions

If GiggleMe converted a member or role into a Discord mention while the scheduled message was being created, users could be pinged immediately.

Instead, GiggleMe stores the placeholder and resolves it at delivery time. People are mentioned only when the scheduled message is actually sent.

2. Resolving against the delivery-time server membership

The member or role needed in the eventual message may not be conveniently selectable from the channel where the schedule is being created.

More importantly, role membership can change between the time a message is scheduled and the time it is delivered.

Resolving the placeholder at delivery time lets GiggleMe use the current guild membership rather than freezing the membership when the schedule is created.

Role expansion

GiggleMe supports the :expand modifier.

{Testers:expand}

When the message is delivered, GiggleMe finds the members who currently have the Testers role and expands the placeholder into mentions for those individual members.

GiggleMe also supports exclusions when expanding roles.

Reliable role expansion requires access to the server's member population rather than relying on whichever members happen to be present in an incomplete local cache.

Example

A scheduled message contains:

Deployment complete. Please review: {Testers:expand}

When the schedule is created, no members of the Testers role are pinged.

At delivery time, if Alice and Bob currently hold the Testers role, GiggleMe resolves the placeholder and sends the equivalent of:

Deployment complete. Please review: @Alice, @Bob

The actual Discord mentions are generated only when the scheduled message is delivered.

Requested access

GiggleMe requests Guild Members so member and role placeholders can be resolved reliably using current guild membership, particularly when expanding roles into their individual members.

GiggleMe does not use the Guild Members intent for advertising, profiling, presence tracking, or unrelated collection of server-member information.
