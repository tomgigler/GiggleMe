GiggleMe structured repeat/duration smoke test
================================================

After applying structured-repeat-duration.patch and restarting GiggleTest:

1. Open /giggle schedule.
   Confirm repeat_unit offers:
     Minutes, Hours, Days, Weeks, Months
   and repeat_every is an integer field.

2. Confirm duration_unit offers:
     Minutes, Hours, Days, Weeks, Months
   and duration_for is an integer field.

3. Schedule:
     repeat_unit: Hours
     repeat_every: 6
     duration_unit: Days
     duration_for: 3

   Verify the stored repeat is hours:6 and the duration is accepted.

4. Schedule:
     repeat_unit: Days
     repeat_every: 2

   Verify the message advances by two calendar days after delivery.

5. Schedule:
     repeat_unit: Weeks
     repeat_every: 2

   Verify the next delivery advances by two weeks.

6. Schedule:
     repeat_unit: Months
     repeat_every: 1

   Verify the next delivery advances by one calendar month.

7. Validation:
   - choose repeat_unit without repeat_every -> useful error
   - provide repeat_every without repeat_unit -> useful error
   - use 0 or a negative amount -> rejected
   - specify duration without a repeating message -> rejected

8. /giggle edit:
   Confirm the same unit + integer fields appear.
   Confirm "Remove repeat" clears repeat.
   Confirm "No duration limit" clears repeat_until.

9. Existing-data compatibility:
   If an existing repeating message stores daily, weekly, or monthly,
   confirm it still displays/delivers/reschedules normally.
